Ficus benjamina 'variegata' Physiological Experiment

Timeline: December 3 2025 – November 3 2026

Overview

This physiological experiment tells the 11-month structural tracking of a variegated weeping fig (Ficus benjamina 'variegata') subjected to environmental stress. The experiment is meant to span 11 months in order to assess resilience and recovery strength of the plant. Experiment has been declared a failure due to a data wipe. More information will be mentioned in Further sections.

Purpose

The core objective of this study was to see how the notoriously 'fussy' Ficus benjamina 'variegata' functions under challanging conditions, allowing us to see how it recovers or collapses.

Background

Research shows that variegated plant varieties are inherently more vulnerable to environmental stress than fully green varieties[1]. This makes my experiment with Ficus benjamina 'variegata' especially critical, as it tests a cultivar known for being uniquely less resilient than a typical weeping fig.

That makes me hypothisize that it wasn't stable to begin with due to the competition of the four specimen.
